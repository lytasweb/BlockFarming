How it works

  1. At the shop, check the label is intact (no VOID marks).
  2. Scratch the silver panel in front of the seller.
  3. Dial *150*00#, choose 1, and enter the 12 digits. You can also SMS the code to 15000 or use this page.
  4. GENUINE? Pay no more than the price shown. Keep the SMS receipt. The code now works only once.
  5. FAKE / ALREADY USED / EXPIRED? Do not pay, and report it.

Official maximum prices (TFRA)

Subsidised price per 50 kg bag. Never pay more.

Report a VOID or broken label

The bag number is printed next to the label (e.g. MM26001-0007). The bag is blocked immediately and TFRA inspectors are notified.

Check a verification reference

Use the ref on your receipt (e.g. BF-1A2B3C) to confirm the sale was recorded on the ledger.
